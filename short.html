AI Researcher

Multi-Agent Systems · Game Theory · Human-Centered AI · Human Behavior Prediction · LLMs & Text Analysis

AI researcher with a Ph.D. in Software and Information Systems Engineering (Ben-Gurion University) and a postdoc at Carnegie Mellon University. Over 8 years taking AI from research to production: fine-tuned LLMs, text embeddings and deep-learning models, predictive ML systems in industry (Amdocs, Diagnostic Robotics), and multi-agent and human-agent decision-making research published at top venues (Best Paper Award, ACM EC; JACM; IJCAI; CHI).

I lead NLP and LLM research at DICTA and run MashInnovateAI, an AI R&D consultancy that helps companies solve their core challenges with AI. I have taught at five institutions, twice received Ben-Gurion University's Excellence in Teaching Award, and serve as a reviewer for AAAI, IJCAI, AAMAS, KDD, CHI, EC, and ECAI.
